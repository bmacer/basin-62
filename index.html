Field manual

How the basin is fought

Orders

  • Left drag selects your units. Shift-click adds or removes.
  • Double-click a unit to grab every visible one of that type.
  • Right-click ground to move. Shift queues another waypoint.
  • Right-click an enemy to attack. A sapper on a building captures it.
  • Right-click your hauler with infantry selected to load. U unloads.
  • A then click: attack-move. T then click: bombard that ground.
  • S stop · G guard · D deploy rig · H find your yard.
  • X sell · R repair · F aim superweapon · Esc cancel.

Base

  • Select the rig and press D, or use Deploy on the sidebar. Structures must touch your existing base.
  • The Base / Foot / Armor / Air / Sea tabs stay up. You do not have to select the building to queue.
  • When a structure finishes, place it on green ground. Drag a fence or berm to lay a line. Right-click refunds.
  • Low power slows production and shuts off guns, flak, coils, radar, and the superweapon.
  • Ore regrows if a vein is not stripped bare. Gems pay more and come back slower.
  • Credit vaults raise the ceiling. Full stores turn haulers away.
  • Radar (survey mast / listening post) turns the minimap on.
  • 1–9 recalls a group. Ctrl+number assigns. Shift+number adds it to the selection.
  • Arrow keys or the screen edge scroll. Wheel zooms. Middle mouse pans. [ ] changes speed. P pauses. Space jumps to the last alert.
